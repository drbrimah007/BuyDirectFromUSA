Global reach without the lift

We connect you to vetted international buyers in the Gulf, sub-Saharan Africa, North Africa, the UK and the EU. You don't open foreign bank accounts. You don't research importers. You don't translate paperwork.

You sell domestically — we handle export

As a U.S.-registered company, every transaction with you is a domestic U.S. sale. You invoice us, ship to our New York warehouse, get paid in USD on net-15 terms. We take care of customs, freight forwarding, end-buyer settlement, and overseas delivery.

Both sides protected

We hold the buyer's payment in escrow, verify product against spec on receipt at our warehouse, and only release funds to you when the deal is clean. If the buyer disputes, we mediate. You get paid for honest work; the buyer gets what they ordered.

How it works for you

From listing to paid in five steps.

  1. 1

    Get listed

    Submit your company info, product catalog (images, SKUs, MOQ, pricing), and any certifications. Our team verifies and activates your listings — usually within 1-2 business days.

  2. 2

    Buyer requests a quote

    When a global buyer needs your product, we route the RFQ to you with quantity, target market, and any compliance specs. You quote your domestic FOB price (in USD) and lead time. No talking to the buyer directly.

  3. 3

    We confirm the order and place it with you

    Once the buyer pays us, you get a signed PO. You produce or ship as you would for any U.S. distributor — invoice us, ship to our New York warehouse, and deliver the documents.

  4. 4

    We inspect, consolidate, and export

    Our team verifies the goods against the PO at receipt, handles customs paperwork, books freight, and ships to the international buyer. You don't deal with carriers, brokers, or customs agents.

  5. 5

    You get paid

    USD payout on standard net-15 terms after receipt and inspection. Net-7 available for verified preferred suppliers. Direct ACH or wire — your choice.
